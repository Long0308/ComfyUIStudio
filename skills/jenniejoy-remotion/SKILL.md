## Explicit subskill contract

- **Input Triggers:** (1) Script and timing known → scaffold and validate title, caption and mix structure immediately; (2) accepted per-shot clips, WAVs, stems or graphics arrive → integrate that hash-bound part and render an incremental preview. Do not wait for every input before starting the work that is already possible.
- **Responsible Owner:** The live root/agent ID recorded in the film PLAN, using this skill's local Remotion compositor route on port 3000. The skill name is not an agent ID.
- **Concrete Output:** Playable per-shot AV previews, 2K 2560×1440 film (downscale of the 4K Master), and mandatory 84-second 4K 3840×2160 Master MP4 (2520 delivery frames at 30fps), accompanied by ffprobe verification and SHA256 receipts.
- **Downstream Result Consumer:** JennieJoy Studio Publisher (`jenniejoy-studio-webapp`) and final independent AV review gate.

Follow the common [engine dispatch contract](../create-video-jenniejoykids/references/engine-dispatch-contract.md). Do not create a competing tracker. Root may perform authorized composition and publication while retaining the single heavy-resource owner; the PLAN-bound Remotion owner executes timeline composition, subtitle typography and audio stem muxing within its assigned scope.

## Timeline, frame rate mapping & output specifications

Active children's production (*Milk and Mom*, `04-milk-and-mom`) uses the following timeline and resolution standards:
- **Duration & Frame Budget:** Exactly 84 seconds = 2520 delivery frames at 30fps.
  - Intro: 3.0 seconds = 90 frames.
  - Story dialogue (12 shots): 69.0 seconds = 2070 frames.
  - Outro & vocabulary flashcards (12 terms): 12.0 seconds = 360 frames.
- **Delivery Resolutions:**
  - **4K Master:** 3840×2160, 30fps, H.264/AAC, high quality profile.
  - **2K Film:** 2560×1440, 30fps, produced by downscaling the finished 4K Master (PLAN T-V10; no unqualified 2K SeedVR2 run exists). Rapid editorial review uses the CPU 1080p review cut (`editorial/review-hd-v003`), not a 2K render.
- **Native 24fps to Delivery 30fps Mapping:** ComfyUI H3 generates native video at 24fps (e.g. 120 used frames = 5.0 seconds). Remotion maps these clips onto the 30fps delivery timeline (150 frames = 5.0 seconds).
- **Audio-Video Synchronization Rule:** Each shot has exactly one dialogue WAV: the WAV named in that shot's H3 preparation receipt (`technical/motion-v00N/<SHOT>/preparation.json` → `wav.sha256`; S01_A: `technical/hybrid-v001/job.json` → `wav_sha256`), whose SHA256 the H3 job receipt must record identically (the receipt WAV); Remotion must bind that same file and start offset. If silence is trimmed, trim **ONCE upstream before H3** so the receipt records the trimmed file; a trim made after H3 is a different file and must never replace the receipt WAV (episode04 S01_A: untrimmed `5d33ad49…`, see `technical/audio-binding-r27/S01_A-WAV-BINDING-DECISION.json`). **Never trim dialogue audio with `startFrom=trim_start_frames` without a paired video picture offset**; doing so advances speech ahead of lips and threatens visual lip-sync desynchronization (see Case 06 in [production case studies](../create-video-jenniejoykids/references/production-case-studies-20261027.md)).
- **Carry Frame Endpoint:** Continuous scene shots require carrying the exact last USED native frame (e.g., native frame 119 for 120 used frames at 24fps). Never truncate a shot early in the timeline while carrying a later native frame.

## Soundtrack separation: music vs. SFX

Remotion owns the final audio stem mix:
1. **Dialogue Stem:** Pure 48kHz speech WAVs from VoiceStudio, bound to exact caption timings.
2. **Music Stem:** Separately sourced background music, loop-adjusted, with automated ducking (-14 dB to -18 dB) beneath dialogue beats.
3. **SFX/Foley Stem:** Environment ambience (morning farm pasture) and discrete Foley spot effects (milk pouring, bucket contact, cow chewing, bottle cap click), aligned exactly to visible video contact frames.

Never merge music into SFX stems or treat an unmixed placeholder as a completed soundtrack.

## End card and tail frames (user decision 2026-10-27)

- The JennieJoy logo ending is a **separate composition** of 60 f (2 s) joined after the main film: total = main 2520 f + end card 60 f = 2580 f / 86 s. Main-film picture and audio (f0–2519) stay unchanged; the end card carries only the logo plate and a music tail (no dialogue). Implemented in review v005 via `end_hold_frames` + `calculateMetadata` and a standalone `MilkMom-EndLogo` composition.
- Tail-only transitions need the outgoing shot's native tail frames; the 4K master needs those frames restored at 4K too (master config refuses master mode when a 4K tail is missing).
- A logo or overlay must never cover a vocabulary word while its pronunciation is playing (confirmed by the v005 critic: the shrinking logo disc hid "Creamy" for f2160–2163 while its WAV played).
- Never cross-dissolve two shots that contain the same characters in different positions: it double-exposes them (v005 f1310–1314: two mothers, two girls). Use a masked wipe (soft directional, cloud/sparkle), a whip, or a carry instead; dissolves are only for changes of place or props without the same character overlapping.
- The same rule covers PROPS (bottle, cups, toys): a masked wipe must never show two copies of one object — never centre a wipe on an object whose position differs between the two shots (v007 f1011: wipe centred on the milk bottle showed two bottles). Open the mask from a region where the two shots agree (sky, background) or where the outgoing shot has no counterpart, or use a whip/cut-on-action when props moved between shots.
- **Film-mix standard (user rule 2026-10-27):** every section of the film sits at −17 ±1 LUFS (true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, ≤ 2 LU jump between sections); music ducks gently under speech (−6…−8 dB, attack 0.1–0.2 s, release 0.5–1 s) and duck windows < ~1.5 s apart are merged so the bed never pumps; measure per section with ebur128 before review. Engine: studio-video-engine `jjv audio mix`.
- **User principle (2026-10-27): effects are a composite layer, never a shot re-render.** Transitions, captions, icons, logo and cards are applied AFTER the shot videos exist; changing a transition = edit its preset in boundaries.json and re-run the composite (Remotion, CPU) only. H3/SeedVR2 shots are re-rendered only when the video itself is broken or characters/props are laid out wrong. Presets that show both shots need tail handles; pick only presets the existing handles support (the kit reports per-boundary feasibility) — a preset that would need new tail frames is refused, never silently turned into GPU work.
- **User rule (2026-10-27, review v012): match the transition to what CHANGES.** Same seating/position and same camera angle, only props or poses change between two lines of dialogue (ep04 T1010, T1310) → NO white flash and no pan: a hard cut with a gentle push-in on the incoming shot (~4% scale easing to 100% over 10–12 f), one shot visible per frame. Only when the camera angle or the scene/location changes (ep04 T1610 tree → bench; T1495 motivated pan) use a dip-to-white/light flash or a camera move/whip. A white flash between two near-identical framings reads as a glitch (user: "không có chuyển cảnh mà làm flash white chi"). Dissolves stay forbidden whenever props/characters are placed differently (double exposure).
- **User pacing rules (2026-10-27, review v012):** the JennieJoy logo after the iris must be fully visible and still for ≥60 f (2 s) before it moves or fades (v012 held it 13 f = 0.43 s: "quá nhanh chưa thấy logo"); every outro vocabulary card lasts ≥90 f (3 s) with its word WAV at the card start (v012: 24–47 f each, "không đủ 3s"). These lengthen the film; the total is derived from the cards, never squeezed to an old frame count, and the music bed is extended without an audible seam.
- Decisive rule (v007 critic, 2026-10-27): when characters or props are placed differently in the two shots, use only transitions that NEVER show both shots in the same frame — dip-to-white/bloom (outgoing brightens to white, incoming emerges from white), a whip pan, or a cut-on-action. Sweeping masks (cloud, bubble, linear wipe) on such pairs showed two Alices (f1315, f1616) and two bottles (f1011); repositioning the mask does not fix the class. Put the white peak no later than the frame before the incoming shot's first spoken word (v008 critic: a peak at B+3 hid the first 2 spoken frames, audio led picture by 70–130 ms); the incoming picture must be visible when the first word starts, with no single-frame luma step that is the largest in the film (v009: white→55% in one frame = −48 luma; v010 uses 0.62/0.35/0.15/0.04 white after the peak — ≈38% picture on the first word, ≈65% one frame later, audio lead ≈33 ms within lip-sync tolerance). Static-hold gates (EndLogo ≥45 f) are measured as 4×4 box-mean abs luma diff vs the last frame ≤ 0.02 — x264 noise makes exact-pixel identity impossible. Any overlay with a scheduled start must be checked for an earlier-mounted layer drawing it (v009: logo visible over the last vocab card two frames before its declared start).

## Review-cut motion layer (user-mandated 2026-10-27, domain `jenniejoy_cartoon_3d`)

Implements gates G-CAP, G-ICON, G-CUT, G-BRIDGE, G-END and G-LABEL (G-MOTION, the technique map, is a brief/critic obligation and is not implemented here) from [create-video-jenniejoykids](../create-video-jenniejoykids/SKILL.md) (error DB: [review-cut-v003-user-fail-motion-transitions-ending.json](../create-video-jenniejoykids/error-db/review-cut-v003-user-fail-motion-transitions-ending.json)). Briefing tables: [brief-template](../opus-motion-engineering/references/brief-template.md). **Master clock invariant: `durationInFrames` stays 2520 @30 fps, and every dialogue WAV keeps its hash and absolute start frame.** Everything below is an overlay or a transform on the existing layers. After building, assert in a pre-render script that each shot's global start frame equals the shot table and the audio start offsets are unchanged; a mismatch is a build failure. Numeric values are starting values, `chưa đo` until measured.

1. **Word-by-word captions.** One caption component takes `words: {w, startF, endF}[]` loaded from the G-CAP word-timing JSON (global frames @30 fps; the JSON SHA256 goes in the RECEIPT). The active word is the last word whose `startF <= useCurrentFrame()`; spoken, active and unspoken words get distinct style tokens from the brief (the pink `#FF2E7E` keyword pill is kept). No `setTimeout`, `Date.now()` or CSS animation. A word missing from the JSON must throw at build time, never fall back silently to a whole-line caption. Fix alignment offsets when building the JSON, never by shifting the WAV in Remotion (the trim-once rule above still wins).
2. **Icon pop.** One component driven by the G-ICON event table: it mounts at the spoken word's `startF` (plus the lead in the brief), scales with `spring` using the brief's duration/damping/stiffness, overshoot kept small, optional sparkle seeded and limited to a few frames, then holds and exits. It stays inside the safe area and clear of the speaking character's face and mouth (use the shot's mouth box). Pop SFX go on the pop frame in the SFX stem.
3. **Transitions at every boundary (G-CUT).** Choose per boundary from the table:
   - **Tail-handle overlap (preferred when handle frames exist):** keep the incoming clip on its original start frame, and let the outgoing clip's unused tail frames (hash-bound, exact native frames after the last USED frame) play underneath it for `k` frames while a wipe mask (iris circle via SVG `clipPath`, or a cartoon cloud-wipe alpha asset from HyperFrames) reveals the incoming picture. Only valid when handle frames >= `k` and the incoming speaker does not start talking before the wipe ends. Implement as manual overlap: two layered `<Sequence>` with absolute `from` frames and an `interpolate`-driven mask.
   - **TransitionSeries is not used for the JennieJoy master: any overlap > 0 shortens the total below 2520 f; transitions are tail-handle overlays inside a fixed 2520 f timeline.** (It also moves later scenes earlier, which desyncs absolute-frame audio.)
   - **Virtual camera move on the source (no handle needed):** at most 6% scale or pan (start value) with a matching push/settle on both sides of the boundary, via `interpolate` only. The clips are native 864x480 upscaled, so larger moves expose softness. Whip-pan: fast pan with a short deterministic blur, sparing and motivated (see the Bad list in the [prompt index](../opus-motion-engineering/references/awesome-opus55-prompt-index.md)).
   - A hard cut stays legal only with a written motivation in the boundary table.
4. **Bridges (G-BRIDGE).** Remotion places a bridge shot but does not invent one: the walking or moving footage comes from the storyboard/H3 lane as a hash-bound clip. If none exists, use a declared motivated transition from item 3 and say so in the table. Never fake movement with a Ken Burns move on a still.
5. **Ending (G-END).** Iris-out on the final story frame, then the JennieJoy logo plate (HyperFrames component with alpha, hash-bound) revealed on the brief's frames and held for the brief's hold (start value 60 f = 2.0 s). The last frame shows the logo. Keep it inside the 2520 f budget unless the PLAN changes the budget (then update every number in the brief, cue list and checks together). Music and SFX tail follow the cue list.
6. **Review-cut quality label.** Show small on-screen text "REVIEW 1080p · native upscaled", mark the file name (e.g. `REVIEW-1080p30-native-upscaled-v004`) and write RECEIPT `quality_label` exactly equal to the on-screen text; put "native 864x480 H3 clips upscaled, not the 4K SeedVR2 master" in a separate `source_note` (gate G-LABEL). Never call the review cut 4K or the Master; "4K" is only for 3840×2160 from the SeedVR2 master.
7. **Before offering the cut:** partial-render every boundary and the final 3 s ([critic-loop §7](../opus-motion-engineering/references/critic-loop.md)) and have an independent critic watch them; the PLAN-bound Remotion owner never grades these gates itself.
